Privacy Policy

Data use

The tool may access Pinterest account data only to provide the features authorized by the account owner, such as viewing boards and saving Pins.

Data storage

Authentication credentials must be handled securely and must not be shared publicly. Any data retention depends on the hosting and application configuration.

Third-party services

The tool may interact with Pinterest and its API, subject to Pinterest's terms and privacy practices.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner through the GitHub repository.
